About Great Smoky Mountains National Park

Great Smoky Mountains National Park is one of the most visited national parks in the United States, known for its mist-covered mountains, rich biodiversity, and preserved Appalachian heritage. Visitors can explore forests, rivers, and historic sites while enjoying outdoor recreation year-round.

Park Highlights and Activities

Hiking Trails

Explore over 800 miles of trails, from beginner-friendly walks to challenging backcountry routes.

Wildlife Viewing

Observe black bears, deer, birds, and native plant species across diverse ecosystems.

Scenic Views

Enjoy panoramic mountain vistas, valleys, and overlooks perfect for photography.

Camping

Waterfalls

Visit popular waterfalls like Laurel Falls and Grotto Falls along accessible trails.

Historic Sites

Discover preserved cabins, churches, and mills that reflect Appalachian history.

Plan Your Visit to the Smoky Mountains

Best Times to Visit

Spring and fall offer mild temperatures and vibrant scenery, while summer is ideal for hiking. Winter provides a quieter experience with fewer crowds and unique landscapes.

What to Pack

  • Plenty of water and snacks
  • Weather-appropriate clothing layers
  • Trail map or GPS device
  • Durable hiking footwear

Visitor Information and Park Details

  • Location: North Carolina and Tennessee, USA
  • Hours: Open daily; most areas accessible from sunrise to sunset
  • Entrance Fees: Check current parking tags and permit requirements
  • Accessibility: Accessible trails, restrooms, and visitor centers available
